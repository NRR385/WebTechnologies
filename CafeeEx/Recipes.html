Featured Italian Cafe Recipes

  1. Classic Italian Espresso

    Start with freshly ground espresso beans. Brew under high pressure for 25-30 seconds to extract a rich, creamy shot.

  2. Tiramisu

    Layer espresso-soaked ladyfingers with mascarpone cream and dust with cocoa powder. Chill for at least 4 hours before serving.

  3. Caprese Panini

    Assemble fresh mozzarella, tomato slices, and basil leaves on rustic bread. Grill until cheese melts and bread is toasted.

  4. Affogato al Caffè

    Pour hot espresso over a scoop of vanilla gelato or ice cream. Serve immediately for a delightful contrast of hot and cold.

  5. Italian Bruschetta

    Toast sliced baguette, rub with garlic, top with diced tomatoes, fresh basil, olive oil, and a pinch of salt.
